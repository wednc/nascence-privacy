Nascence Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

Nascence is a private companion for your cycle and the life stages around it. This policy explains what happens to what you record in the Android app. In short, it stays on your phone.

What Nascence keeps, and where

Everything you enter stays on your phone, in the app's own private storage. That includes your daily reflections, period dates, temperatures, check-ins, appointments, notes and settings.

  • No account. You never sign up or sign in.
  • No server. Nascence has no server of its own and no online service. It does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything over the internet.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking. Nascence contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • No cloud backup. Nascence asks Android to leave your data out of Google's cloud backup, so it is not copied to Google Drive.

We (the developer) never receive, see or store anything you record.

The ways your data can leave your phone

Your data leaves your phone only when you choose to move it. There are two ways to do that.

  1. Moving to a new phone. When you set up a new Android phone, you can copy apps and data from the old one, by cable or directly between the phones. If you do this, Nascence's data comes across with everything else. This is kept on purpose, so your history can follow you. It goes from your phone to your phone, not through us or through Google's cloud backup.
  2. Export. In Self → Data → Export my data, Nascence makes one file (a spreadsheet-style CSV file) of what you have recorded and opens your phone's share menu. You choose where it goes, for example a file on your phone, your own email or your own cloud storage. Once you share it, the file is yours. It is then protected by wherever you put it, not by Nascence. Nascence clears its own temporary copy of the file the next time you export, or when you open the app more than an hour later.

You can bring an exported file back with Restore from a file (in Self → Data) or with Restoring from another phone? on the welcome screen. The file is read on your phone and is not sent anywhere.

Health Connect

If you choose to, Nascence can import your period history from Health Connect, Android's on-phone health store, where other period apps may share it.

  • Nascence asks for one permission only: to read menstruation records. It cannot read any other kind of health data.
  • Read-only. Nascence never writes to, changes or deletes anything in Health Connect.
  • It reads only when you tap Connect Health Connect or Sync now (in Self → Privacy). It does not read in the background.
  • What it reads is used for one thing: to add your period start dates to your Nascence history on your phone. It is never sent anywhere, shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • You can turn the permission off at any time in Health Connect's own settings. Periods already imported stay in Nascence until you remove them or use Delete everything.

Nascence's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Reminders and the home-screen widget

  • Reminders are made on your phone. Nascence schedules them itself; nothing is sent from a server. You can turn each one on or off in Self.
  • Discreet notifications (on unless you turn them off) keep reminder text off your lock screen until you unlock your phone.
  • The home-screen widget is drawn on your phone. Discreet widget (on unless you turn it off) shows less on your home screen.

Keeping it private on your phone

  • You can lock Nascence with a 4-digit PIN, or with your fingerprint or face. Your PIN is stored in scrambled form, not as the digits themselves. A 4-digit PIN is a light lock for privacy from people nearby, not strong encryption.
  • While a lock is on, Nascence hides its preview in the recent-apps screen.
  • Nascence does not add its own encryption to the data file. Your phone's own built-in encryption protects it while your phone is locked. Nascence's lock keeps people out of the app; it does not encrypt the file.

Deleting your data

  • You can delete any single entry or period date in the app.
  • Self → Data → Delete everything erases everything Nascence holds on your phone: your history, settings, lock, reminders, the widget's picture and any export copies Nascence made. If you have a lock, you are asked for it first. This cannot be undone.
  • Uninstalling Nascence also removes everything it stored on your phone.

Delete everything does not reach copies you have made yourself, such as an export file you saved or sent somewhere. It also does not change Health Connect, which belongs to your phone, not to Nascence.

Because we never receive your data, we have nothing to delete on our side.

Children

Nascence is not made for children under 13.
